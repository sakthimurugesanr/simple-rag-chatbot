# Northstar employee handbook

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests must be approved by a manager at least 7 days in advance.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
The office is open Monday through Friday, from 09:00 to 18:00.

## Equipment
The annual home-office equipment allowance is INR 15,000.
Contact the IT team to request a company laptop.
